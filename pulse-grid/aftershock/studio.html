A tiny energy creature escapes a miniature toy factory by switching between three bodies. Pull back, preview a trajectory, release. Spring over rails, break glass with steel, and recover caged energy with a magnet.

What is playable now

Three designed rooms, an alternative route in room three, undo, checkpoint recovery, best launch counts, touch and mouse controls, optional synthesized sound, Hebrew and English, and reduced-motion settings. WebGL rendering includes a software-rendered fallback. No account, paywall, ads, or analytics SDK is required for this build.
